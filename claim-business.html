Business Ownership

Claim a Business

Request access to manage a business listed on NearAfrica. Ownership claims require verification through the registered business email.

Important: The verification code must be sent to the email address already registered to the selected business. Your personal contact email is not a substitute for that verification. Verifying an email does not automatically approve a claim.

1. Find your business

Search for the exact business listing you want to claim.

2. Submit your claim

Confirm the selected listing and provide your contact details.

Enter an email address where NearAfrica can contact you about this claim. The ownership verification code must still be sent to the registered email address for the selected business.

3. Verify the business email

Enter the verification code sent to the email address registered to the selected business.

Security reminder: Use the code sent to the registered business email. Your contact email is not a substitute for business-email verification.

Enter the code exactly as received. The server will determine whether the code is valid.

Verification response received

If your claim still requires review, wait for confirmation from NearAfrica before assuming you have management access.
