Trust & Safety

Report a Listing

Help us keep NearAfrica accurate, trustworthy, and useful by reporting business listings that contain incorrect, misleading, unauthorized, inappropriate, or potentially harmful information.

Important A report does not automatically mean that a listing is false, illegal, fraudulent, or inappropriate. NearAfrica may review the information provided, compare available evidence, contact relevant parties where appropriate, and take action based on the circumstances.

1. Purpose of the Report System

NearAfrica provides a reporting process so that users, businesses, owners, representatives, and other affected persons can bring potentially inaccurate or problematic listings to our attention.

The purpose of the report system is to help maintain accurate directory information and provide a reasonable process for corrections, investigations, disputes, and removal requests.

2. What You Can Report

You may report a listing when you reasonably believe that something about it requires review.

  • Incorrect business information
  • A business that appears to be permanently closed
  • Duplicate listings
  • A business that appears to be fake or unverifiable
  • Impersonation or unauthorized representation
  • Potentially fraudulent information
  • Misleading business information
  • Incorrect contact information
  • Incorrect location information
  • Unauthorized or problematic images
  • Privacy-related concerns
  • Copyright or intellectual-property concerns
  • Reviews or content that may violate NearAfrica rules
  • Other information that you believe requires administrative review

3. Common Report Types

Examples of issues that may be reported include:

Incorrect Information

The business name, address, phone number, category, website, hours, or another important detail appears to be wrong.

Duplicate Listing

The same business appears to have multiple NearAfrica listings.

Fake or Unverifiable

You have a reasonable basis to believe the listed business may not exist or cannot be reasonably verified.

Impersonation

A person appears to be representing a business without appropriate authorization.

Fraud Concern

The listing appears connected to potentially deceptive or fraudulent activity.

Privacy Concern

Personal information appears to have been published or handled inappropriately.

Copyright Concern

An image, logo, description, or other material may have been used without appropriate rights.

Removal Request

You believe a listing should be reviewed for possible correction, restriction, or removal.

4. Before Submitting a Report

Please provide information that you reasonably believe is accurate.

If the issue is simply an outdated business detail, a correction request may be more appropriate than a fraud report.

If you are a business owner or authorized representative, you may also want to use the NearAfrica claim process where applicable.

You can learn more on our Claim Business page.

5. Information to Provide

A useful report should identify the listing and clearly explain the problem.

Where appropriate, provide:

  • Business name
  • NearAfrica business URL or listing ID
  • The specific information you believe is wrong
  • An explanation of why you believe it is wrong
  • Relevant dates
  • Supporting evidence that you are legally entitled to provide
  • Your preferred contact information if follow-up is required

Do not send passwords, payment-card details, security credentials, or unnecessary sensitive personal information through a listing report.

6. How Reports Are Reviewed

Reports may be reviewed by NearAfrica administrators or other authorized personnel.

1

Report received

The report is recorded for administrative review.

2

Information reviewed

The reported listing and the information supplied with the report may be examined.

3

Additional information may be requested

NearAfrica may request clarification or supporting evidence where reasonably necessary.

4

Administrative action

The listing may be corrected, restricted, reviewed further, marked for investigation, or otherwise handled according to the circumstances.

7. Possible Actions

Depending on the nature of the report and the available evidence, NearAfrica may:

  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request additional information
  • Contact a business or claimant
  • Temporarily restrict information while it is reviewed
  • Remove duplicate information
  • Remove or restrict content that violates applicable NearAfrica rules
  • Reject a report when there is insufficient basis for action
  • Escalate an issue for additional administrative or legal review where appropriate

NearAfrica does not guarantee that every report will result in removal or another particular outcome.

8. Urgent or Serious Concerns

NearAfrica is a business-directory platform and should not be treated as an emergency service.

If a matter involves an immediate threat to someone's safety, contact the appropriate emergency service or relevant authority directly.

A report to NearAfrica does not replace a police report, court proceeding, regulatory complaint, or other official process.

9. Personal Information

Information submitted through a report may contain personal information.

NearAfrica should collect and use only information reasonably necessary to receive, investigate, administer, and respond to the report.

Personal information should not be included in a report unnecessarily, particularly information that is sensitive or unrelated to the issue being reported.

For more information about how personal information is handled, please review our Privacy Policy .

10. False, Abusive, or Malicious Reports

The reporting system must not be used to harass, threaten, intimidate, sabotage, or unfairly harm a business or another person.

Examples of problematic use may include:

  • Knowingly submitting false information
  • Repeatedly reporting a listing without a genuine basis
  • Attempting to interfere with a competitor
  • Using reports to threaten a business
  • Submitting fabricated evidence
  • Attempting to manipulate a business's reputation

NearAfrica may restrict reporting privileges or take other appropriate action where there is evidence of abuse.

11. Business Response

Where appropriate, NearAfrica may allow a business owner or authorized representative to respond to, clarify, or challenge information associated with a report.

A business may be asked to provide evidence when the issue involves ownership, authorization, identity, inaccurate information, or another matter requiring verification.

A response from a business does not automatically determine the outcome of a report.

12. Removal Requests

A person may request that a listing or specific information be reviewed for possible removal.

NearAfrica may consider factors including:

  • Whether the listing exists
  • Whether the information is materially inaccurate
  • Whether the content violates NearAfrica policy
  • Whether the requester has authority over the affected information
  • Whether there are legitimate reasons to retain the information
  • Whether removal is appropriate under applicable law

A request for removal is reviewed on its circumstances and does not automatically result in deletion.

13. Copyright and Intellectual Property

If you believe a photo, logo, description, or another piece of content on NearAfrica infringes your copyright or other intellectual-property rights, explain the specific material involved and provide information supporting your complaint.

Where possible, identify the original work, explain your relationship to it, and provide the relevant NearAfrica listing.

Do not submit confidential information that is not necessary to evaluate the complaint.

Copyright complaints may be handled separately from ordinary listing corrections because different evidence and legal considerations may apply.

14. Submit a Report

The NearAfrica reporting form/API can be connected to this policy page so reports are stored for administrative review.

Until the reporting form is connected, you can contact NearAfrica directly with the relevant listing and details of the issue.

NearAfrica Support

Include the business name, listing URL or ID, the problem you identified, and any relevant supporting information.

Please do not send unnecessary passwords, financial credentials, or sensitive personal information.
