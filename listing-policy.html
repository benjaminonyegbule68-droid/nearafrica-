Listing Policy

This policy explains what may be listed on NearAfrica, how listings are reviewed and managed, and how businesses and users can request corrections, report problems, or request removal.

1. Purpose of This Policy

NearAfrica is a business discovery and directory platform designed to help users discover businesses and opportunities across Africa.

This Listing Policy establishes standards for businesses, organizations, users, submitted content, public-source information, and directory listings.

A listing on NearAfrica is not automatically an endorsement, certification, recommendation, or guarantee by NearAfrica.

2. Eligible Listings

NearAfrica may list legitimate businesses, organizations, professionals, services, venues, institutions, and other entities that reasonably fit the purpose of the platform.

Depending on the category and applicable law, this may include:

  • Restaurants and food businesses
  • Hotels and accommodation providers
  • Beauty and spa businesses
  • Health and medical businesses
  • Shops and retailers
  • Supermarkets
  • Banks and financial businesses
  • Professional service providers
  • Educational institutions
  • Real-estate businesses
  • Automotive businesses
  • Travel and tourism businesses
  • Logistics and delivery businesses
  • Technology businesses
  • Entertainment businesses
  • Fitness and sports businesses
  • Construction and home-service providers
  • Fashion businesses
  • Agricultural businesses
  • Religious organizations
  • Government and public-service organizations
  • Other legitimate organizations or services that reasonably fit the platform.

3. Prohibited Listings

NearAfrica may reject, remove, restrict, or suspend listings that involve unlawful, deceptive, harmful, fraudulent, or otherwise prohibited activity.

Examples include:

  • Fake or completely fabricated businesses presented as real.
  • Fraudulent businesses or deceptive schemes.
  • Listings impersonating another business or person.
  • Listings containing knowingly false information.
  • Listings promoting unlawful goods or services.
  • Listings designed primarily to facilitate scams, fraud, or abuse.
  • Listings containing malware, malicious code, or harmful links.
  • Listings that unlawfully expose sensitive personal information.
  • Listings containing material that violates the rights of third parties.
  • Spam or mass-created duplicate listings.

This list is not exhaustive. NearAfrica may take reasonable action against listings that create significant legal, security, safety, or platform integrity concerns.

4. Listing Information

A listing may contain information such as:

  • Business name
  • Category and subcategory
  • Description
  • Address
  • City
  • State or region
  • Country
  • Telephone number
  • WhatsApp number
  • Email address
  • Website
  • Social-media links
  • Business hours
  • Geographic coordinates
  • Photographs or logos
  • Reviews
  • Listing status

NearAfrica may limit, modify, correct, or remove information where reasonably necessary to maintain directory quality, comply with applicable law, or protect users and third parties.

5. Publicly Available and Third-Party Information

NearAfrica may discover or receive business information from public sources, users, businesses, third-party services, or other legitimate sources.

Information being publicly available does not mean that every piece of information is permanently accurate, complete, current, or suitable for publication.

NearAfrica may review, update, correct, restrict, or remove information when appropriate.

NearAfrica does not guarantee that every publicly available business detail will always be accurate or current.

6. Listing Review and Moderation

NearAfrica may review submitted or discovered listings before publication or after publication.

Review may consider factors including:

  • Whether the business appears to exist.
  • Whether submitted information appears internally consistent.
  • Whether the listing duplicates an existing listing.
  • Whether the listing violates this policy.
  • Whether a report or complaint has been received.
  • Whether information requires clarification.

Moderation does not guarantee that a listing is completely accurate or that the business is legally licensed, financially sound, safe, reputable, or suitable for a particular user.

7. Listing Statuses

NearAfrica may use different statuses to communicate the state of a listing.

Unclaimed

The business has not been successfully claimed by an authorized representative through NearAfrica's claim process.

Claimed

A claim has been accepted or associated with the listing through NearAfrica's claim process.

Verified

NearAfrica has completed a defined verification process. Verification does not mean government certification or endorsement.

NearAfrica may change listing statuses when new information becomes available.

8. Business Claims

A person may request to claim a business listing where they are authorized to represent that business.

NearAfrica may request evidence reasonably necessary to assess the claim.

A claim may be rejected where:

  • The claimant cannot reasonably demonstrate authorization.
  • The information conflicts with available evidence.
  • Another claimant has a stronger or previously established claim.
  • The claim appears fraudulent or abusive.
  • Additional information is required.

Claiming a listing does not automatically grant ownership of the NearAfrica listing itself or of information belonging to third parties.

9. Verification

Where NearAfrica provides verification, the verification process may involve checking information supplied by the business or other available evidence.

The exact verification method may differ depending on the business and the information available.

A NearAfrica verification badge must not be interpreted as a government license, professional accreditation, safety certification, financial guarantee, or general endorsement.

10. Duplicate Listings

NearAfrica may merge, remove, redirect, or otherwise manage duplicate listings.

Duplicate listings may be created accidentally, through multiple submissions, business relocations, changes of name, or other circumstances.

Where appropriate, NearAfrica may preserve relevant information while consolidating duplicate records.

11. Accuracy and Correction Requests

Businesses and users may report inaccurate, outdated, incomplete, or misleading information.

A correction request should provide enough information for NearAfrica to identify the listing and understand the requested correction.

NearAfrica may request supporting evidence where appropriate.

NearAfrica may not accept every requested change automatically.

12. Business Removal Requests

A business or authorized representative may request removal of a listing.

Removal requests may be reviewed to determine:

  • Whether the requester has authority to make the request.
  • Whether the listing should remain available for legitimate directory purposes.
  • Whether legal or safety considerations apply.
  • Whether the information can instead be corrected or restricted.

NearAfrica may retain limited records concerning the listing or removal request where reasonably necessary for legal, security, dispute-resolution, or administrative purposes.

13. Reports and Complaints

Users may report listings that they believe violate NearAfrica policies or contain serious inaccuracies.

Reports may concern:

  • Fraud or suspected scams.
  • Impersonation.
  • Incorrect business information.
  • Duplicate listings.
  • Copyright concerns.
  • Privacy concerns.
  • Inappropriate content.
  • Other policy violations.

Submitting a report does not guarantee that a listing will be removed.

Knowingly submitting false or abusive reports may itself violate NearAfrica's Terms of Service.

14. Reviews

Reviews should reflect genuine experiences and should not be fabricated, manipulated, or submitted for the purpose of misleading users.

Reviews must not:

  • Impersonate another person.
  • Contain deliberately fabricated experiences.
  • Be created through coordinated manipulation.
  • Contain threats or unlawful harassment.
  • Expose unnecessary private information.
  • Contain unlawful or prohibited material.

NearAfrica may moderate, restrict, remove, or retain reviews according to its policies and applicable law.

A business may report a review it believes violates the rules.

15. Images, Logos, and Copyright

Users and businesses must not upload photographs, logos, graphics, or other material unless they have the necessary rights or authorization to provide that material.

NearAfrica may remove or restrict material when it receives a credible copyright or rights-related complaint.

NearAfrica may also preserve relevant records when reasonably necessary to investigate a dispute.

If you believe content on NearAfrica infringes your rights, contact us with enough information to identify the material and explain the basis of your complaint.

16. Listing Ranking and Featured Placement

NearAfrica may organize listings using factors such as relevance, category, location, listing quality, completeness, activity, verification status, and other platform factors.

NearAfrica may introduce paid or promotional placement features in the future.

Where paid placement is introduced, NearAfrica may identify promotional or featured placements where appropriate.

Paid placement does not automatically mean that a business is verified, endorsed, or guaranteed by NearAfrica.

17. Suspension, Restriction, or Removal

NearAfrica may suspend, restrict, hide, modify, merge, or remove a listing where reasonably necessary.

Reasons may include:

  • Violation of this Listing Policy.
  • Violation of the Terms of Service.
  • Fraud or suspected fraudulent activity.
  • Impersonation.
  • Significant inaccuracies.
  • Repeated abuse of platform systems.
  • Legal or regulatory requirements.
  • Security or safety concerns.

NearAfrica may restore or modify a listing when sufficient information becomes available to justify doing so.

18. Legal Requests

NearAfrica may respond to valid legal requests, court orders, regulatory requirements, law-enforcement requests, or other legally binding processes where required or permitted by applicable law.

Depending on the circumstances, NearAfrica may preserve relevant records while a matter is being investigated or resolved.

19. Contact NearAfrica

For listing corrections, removal requests, reports, copyright concerns, verification questions, or other listing-related matters, contact NearAfrica.
