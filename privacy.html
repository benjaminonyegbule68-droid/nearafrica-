1. Introduction

NearAfrica is a business-discovery and directory platform intended to help people discover businesses and opportunities across Africa.

We take privacy seriously and aim to handle personal information responsibly, transparently, and only for legitimate purposes connected with operating and improving NearAfrica.

This policy applies to information processed through NearAfrica websites, forms, business listings, claims, reports, reviews, administrative systems, and related services.

2. Who We Are

NearAfrica operates the NearAfrica directory and related services.

For privacy questions, requests, corrections, complaints, or data-protection concerns, contact:

NearAfrica Privacy Contact

Contact Privacy Support

3. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use NearAfrica, the information we process may include:

Contact Information

Name, email address, telephone number, WhatsApp number, and other contact information you voluntarily provide.

Business Information

Business name, address, category, city, state, country, website, business description, operating information, and related details.

Claim Information

Information supplied when claiming or verifying a business listing.

Report Information

Information submitted when reporting a listing, review, image, privacy concern, or other issue.

Review Information

Name or display information, ratings, comments, and other information submitted with a review.

Location Information

Business location information and, where a feature specifically requests it, location information supplied by the user.

Images & Files

Images, logos, documents, or other files you voluntarily submit where a feature allows them.

Technical Information

IP address, browser information, device information, timestamps, pages visited, and technical logs where collected by our infrastructure or service providers.

4. Where Information Comes From

Information may come from several sources, including:

  • Information you provide directly
  • Business owners or authorized representatives
  • Users who submit listings, reviews, or reports
  • Publicly available business information
  • Third-party data sources used for directory discovery
  • Our technology and hosting providers
  • Administrative and security systems

Public business information may be collected or processed for directory purposes, but NearAfrica does not intend to represent that every piece of third-party information is permanently accurate.

5. Why We Use Information

NearAfrica may process information for purposes including:

  • Creating and displaying business listings
  • Processing business submissions
  • Processing business claims and verification
  • Reviewing reports and complaints
  • Publishing and moderating reviews
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, spam, and manipulation
  • Maintaining platform security
  • Measuring usage and improving the platform
  • Responding to support requests
  • Communicating with users where necessary
  • Maintaining administrative records
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations
  • Protecting NearAfrica, users, businesses, and other affected persons

6. Lawful Basis for Processing

Where applicable, NearAfrica will process personal information on a lawful basis recognized by applicable data-protection law.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

  • Consent
  • Performance of a requested service or agreement
  • Compliance with a legal obligation
  • Protection of legitimate interests where permitted by law
  • Other lawful grounds recognized by applicable law

The appropriate lawful basis depends on the specific processing activity and the circumstances in which information is collected.

7. Public Business Directory Information

NearAfrica is a directory. Some business information may therefore be displayed publicly.

Public information may include:

  • Business name
  • Business category
  • General business location
  • Business address
  • Business telephone number
  • Business website
  • Business social-media links
  • Business description
  • Business images and logos where permitted
  • Reviews and ratings submitted through NearAfrica
  • Listing status such as Unclaimed, Claimed, or Verified

Information intended to identify a business should not automatically be understood as personal information belonging to an individual. However, where business information relates to an identifiable individual, applicable privacy obligations may still apply.

8. Business Listings and Submissions

When you submit a business to NearAfrica, we may process the information required to review and publish the listing.

This may include your contact information so that NearAfrica can communicate with you about the submission where necessary.

You are responsible for ensuring that information you submit is accurate and that you have the appropriate authority or rights to submit material that you provide.

Additional rules are described in our Terms of Service and Listing Policy .

9. Business Claims and Verification

When someone attempts to claim a business listing, NearAfrica may collect information needed to evaluate whether the claimant is authorized to represent the business.

Depending on the claim process, this may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Relationship to the business
  • Verification information
  • Supporting evidence where reasonably necessary

NearAfrica should request only information that is reasonably necessary for the claim or verification process.

Claim approval is an administrative verification process. It is not a court determination of legal ownership.

10. Reports and Complaints

When you submit a report, NearAfrica may process the information you provide to investigate the issue and determine whether administrative action is appropriate.

Reports may relate to inaccurate information, duplicate listings, impersonation, fraud concerns, privacy issues, copyright complaints, inappropriate content, or removal requests.

Information associated with a report may be retained as an administrative record where reasonably necessary for security, dispute handling, legal compliance, or audit purposes.

11. Reviews and Ratings

If NearAfrica provides review functionality, the information you submit may be publicly displayed according to the applicable review system.

Reviews may include a display name, rating, comment, date, and other information associated with the review.

Reviews may be moderated, restricted, or removed where they violate applicable NearAfrica policies.

NearAfrica may retain moderation records to help prevent repeated abuse or manipulation.

12. Cookies and Technical Information

NearAfrica may use cookies, local storage, server logs, or similar technologies where necessary for functionality, security, preferences, analytics, or other legitimate platform purposes.

Technical information may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Operating-system information
  • Referring pages
  • Pages or resources requested
  • Date and time of requests
  • Security and error logs

The exact technologies used may change as the platform develops.

13. Third-Party Service Providers

NearAfrica may use third-party technology providers to operate parts of the platform.

These may include providers for:

  • Website hosting
  • Database infrastructure
  • Image and file storage
  • Analytics
  • Security and abuse prevention
  • Email or communications
  • Payments and subscriptions
  • Maps and location services

Providers may process information only to the extent reasonably necessary to provide the relevant service or as otherwise permitted by applicable law.

14. When Information May Be Shared

NearAfrica does not intend to sell personal information simply because someone uses the directory.

Information may nevertheless be disclosed where reasonably necessary for legitimate purposes, including:

  • Publishing information that you intentionally submit for public display
  • Using service providers that help operate NearAfrica
  • Processing payments or subscriptions
  • Investigating fraud or abuse
  • Protecting users or the platform
  • Responding to lawful requests from authorities
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations
  • Handling disputes, claims, or legal proceedings

NearAfrica will seek to limit disclosures to what is reasonably appropriate for the relevant purpose, subject to applicable law.

15. Security

NearAfrica will use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

Security measures may include:

  • Access controls
  • Administrative authentication
  • Secure transport where supported
  • Database access restrictions
  • Administrative logs
  • Security monitoring and abuse controls

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should therefore avoid submitting unnecessary confidential information.

16. Data Retention

NearAfrica may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected or for other legitimate purposes such as security, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, accounting, legal compliance, or maintaining administrative records.

Different categories of information may therefore have different retention periods.

Where information is no longer reasonably required, NearAfrica may delete, anonymize, or otherwise dispose of it subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Depending on the circumstances, these may include rights relating to:

  • Access to personal information
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion or erasure where legally applicable

  • Withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent
  • Data portability where applicable
  • Complaints concerning personal-data processing

These rights are not absolute and may be subject to legal exceptions, legitimate interests, security requirements, or other applicable restrictions.

If a feature involves age restrictions or additional consent requirements, those requirements will apply to that feature.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to NearAfrica in circumstances where it should not have been collected, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

Where applicable, NearAfrica will take reasonable steps required by applicable law when information is transferred or processed across borders.

The countries and providers involved may change as NearAfrica's infrastructure develops.
