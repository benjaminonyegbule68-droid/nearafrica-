1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to information processed through NearAfrica's website, directory, business submission tools, claim system, review features, reporting tools, contact channels, and other services that link to this Privacy Policy.

It explains how NearAfrica may collect, use, store, protect, and disclose personal information.

NearAfrica aims to handle personal information in a fair, lawful, transparent, and security-conscious manner and in accordance with applicable data protection law.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use NearAfrica, we may collect different types of information.

Information You Provide

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • WhatsApp number
  • Business information
  • Business address
  • Business description
  • Website and social-media information
  • Photos, logos, and other uploaded content
  • Claim information
  • Review information
  • Report and correction information
  • Messages sent to NearAfrica

Information Generated Through Use

  • Pages viewed
  • Searches and interactions
  • Business listing views
  • Approximate technical information
  • Browser and device information
  • Security and diagnostic information

3. Business Information

When a user submits a business to NearAfrica, information about that business may be stored and displayed as part of the directory.

Some business information may be publicly visible, including information such as business name, category, location, contact details, website, description, photographs, social-media information, listing status, and reviews.

If you submit information about another person, business, or organization, you should have a legitimate basis or authorization to provide that information where required by applicable law.

4. Technical Information

When you access NearAfrica, technical information may be processed to operate, secure, maintain, and improve the platform.

Depending on the technology in use, this may include:

  • IP address or network information
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Approximate time of access
  • Pages or resources requested
  • Referrer information
  • Error and security logs

We may use this information to detect abuse, troubleshoot errors, understand platform usage, improve performance, and protect NearAfrica.

5. Location Information

NearAfrica may process business location information such as addresses, cities, states, countries, and geographic coordinates associated with business listings.

Where a feature allows a user to voluntarily provide device location to help identify or select a business location, that information will be processed for the purpose explained by the relevant feature.

NearAfrica does not need your precise personal location merely for you to browse ordinary business listings unless a particular feature specifically requires it and you choose to use that feature.

6. How We Use Information

NearAfrica may use information for purposes including:

  • Providing and operating the directory.
  • Processing business submissions.
  • Processing business claims.
  • Displaying business listings.
  • Displaying reviews.
  • Processing reports and correction requests.
  • Communicating with users.
  • Responding to support requests.
  • Preventing fraud and abuse.
  • Protecting platform security.
  • Maintaining administrative records.
  • Understanding platform usage.
  • Improving NearAfrica.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

7. Legal Bases for Processing

Depending on the circumstances, NearAfrica may rely on one or more lawful bases recognized by applicable data-protection law.

These may include:

  • Your consent.
  • Performance of a contract or steps requested before entering into a contract.
  • Compliance with a legal obligation.
  • Protection of legitimate interests where permitted by applicable law.
  • Protection of vital interests where applicable.
  • Other lawful bases recognized by applicable law.

The appropriate legal basis depends on the specific processing activity and applicable law.

8. When Information May Be Shared

NearAfrica may share information where reasonably necessary for legitimate platform operations and where permitted by applicable law.

This may include sharing information with:

  • Hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Database and storage providers.
  • Map and location-service providers.
  • Security and technical service providers.
  • Communications providers.
  • Payment providers when payment features are introduced.
  • Professional advisers where appropriate.
  • Law-enforcement or regulatory authorities where legally required or permitted.

NearAfrica does not intend to sell personal information merely because you use the directory.

9. Information That May Become Public

NearAfrica is a public business directory.

Information intentionally submitted for publication may become publicly accessible.

This may include business names, business contact information, addresses, descriptions, websites, social-media links, photographs, business locations, listing statuses, and reviews.

Do not submit private information that you do not want displayed publicly.

This is especially important when submitting business descriptions, photographs, reviews, or contact information.

10. Storage and Security

NearAfrica may store information using third-party infrastructure and service providers.

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

Security measures may include access controls, authentication, administrative controls, secure infrastructure, logging, monitoring, and other measures appropriate to the nature of the information.

However, no internet service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

NearAfrica may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected and for legitimate operational, security, legal, accounting, dispute-resolution, and recordkeeping purposes.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

When information is no longer reasonably required, NearAfrica may delete, anonymize, or otherwise securely dispose of it where appropriate and technically feasible.

12. Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to applicable law and relevant conditions, you may have rights concerning your personal information.

These may include rights to:

  • Be informed about processing.
  • Request access to personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion or erasure where applicable.
  • Object to certain processing.
  • Request restriction of processing in applicable circumstances.
  • Request data portability where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where consent is the applicable legal basis.
  • Exercise other rights provided by applicable data-protection law.

These rights are not absolute and may be subject to lawful exceptions or conditions.

The Nigeria Data Protection Commission identifies data-subject rights including access, rectification, objection, restriction, portability, erasure, and related rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act.

13. Cookies and Similar Technologies

NearAfrica or its service providers may use cookies, local storage, scripts, pixels, or similar technologies where necessary to operate features, maintain preferences, understand usage, improve performance, or protect the platform.

The technologies actually used may change as NearAfrica develops.

Where applicable, users may control certain browser or device settings relating to cookies and similar technologies.

Disabling certain technologies may affect some features of the platform.

14. Children and Young Users

NearAfrica is not intended to require children to provide unnecessary personal information.

Where applicable law requires parental or guardian involvement for certain processing or agreements, NearAfrica will seek to operate consistently with those requirements.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to NearAfrica in circumstances where this should not have occurred, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

15. International Processing

Some of NearAfrica's technical service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live.

Where personal information is transferred or processed across borders, NearAfrica intends to apply safeguards required by applicable data-protection law.

16. Data Incidents and Security Breaches

If NearAfrica becomes aware of a personal-data incident that requires action under applicable law, NearAfrica will assess and respond to the incident in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include investigating the incident, taking steps to contain it, improving security measures, documenting the incident, and making notifications where legally required.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

NearAfrica may update this Privacy Policy as the platform, technology, legal requirements, or data practices change.

Material changes may be communicated through the website or another reasonable method.

The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was most recently updated.
